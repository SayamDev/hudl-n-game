# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion, Zustand, canvas-confetti, vite-plugin-pwa, lz-string. Local browser APIs and self-hosted fonts.
## Users
Families and friends gathered around one shared phone, with tablets and laptops also supported.
## Product Purpose
Hudl N Game is a free, ad-free, account-free pass-and-play party game collection. Pick a game, add 3-20 names, and start quickly.
## Operating Context
Portrait-first browser play. The phone passes between players. Imposter secrets hide immediately when a hold ends. Other private cards remain open for reading until explicitly hidden/passed, interrupted or paused. Play must work offline after assets are cached.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Seven lobby choices: Imposter, Mafia, Spyfall, Secret Hitler, Heads Up, Charades, and Most Likely To / Hot Takes. Persist local sessions and scores, create and share custom packs, provide accessible settings and narrator controls. No accounts, analytics, tracking cookies, or paid fallback. An optional server-side Groq connector generates pack drafts only when requested; gameplay and curated mixes remain offline. Family mode defaults on; adult content requires explicit opt-in. The public app is hosted on GitHub Pages; optional server AI generation requires separately configured hosting.
## Brand Commitments
The user revised the initial visual brief after finding it too babyish. Social Club is now the default: mature party-deck styling, lime/charcoal with softer secondary game colours, Space Grotesk, a geometric HUDL logo, compact phone lobby and thumb-reachable navigation. Explicit private IMPOSTER role label, full-height phone play and easy touch targets take priority. Classic preserves the original neo-pop sticker-book palette, Fredoka/Nunito, outlined cards and mascot, selectable in Settings without resetting data. Player colours and emoji avatars remain part of shared-device identification. Tagline: Huddle up. Pass the phone. Cause chaos.
## Evidence on Hand
Full user-provided product brief in the attachment. Fresh repository without existing source or remote. No usage claims or testimonials.
## Product Principles
1. Make shared-device play immediate.
2. Protect secret reveals during handoffs.
3. Keep the full game usable offline.
4. Prioritize phone touch controls and smooth rendering.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA, visible focus, semantic controls, keyboard play, reduced motion, high contrast, scalable text, text narration fallback, and markers that never depend on color alone.

## Identity refinement
The user requested recognisable custom avatars, game-specific artwork, visible animation, a larger “n game” wordmark, a distinctive round setup and © Sayam attribution. Social Club now extends its existing palette into geometric masked identities and physical game-deck motifs. Both previous visual variants remain selectable. Avatar names, privacy, family controls and reduced-motion support retain priority over decoration.

## Distinct game worlds
The latest refinement replaces shared pastel deck styling with distinct game palettes and emblems: acid-green Imposter, midnight/gold Mafia, green Spy dossier, coral Heads Up stage, yellow/purple Most Likely To. Shared controls and privacy behavior remain consistent. Home shows the actual twenty-member alibi cast via distinct silhouettes/expressions/accessories; decorative animation replay controls are removed. Earlier design options remain accessible.
