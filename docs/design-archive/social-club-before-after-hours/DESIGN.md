# Design System: Hudl N Game

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Social Club game deck"**

A mature, playful party identity built from lime and charcoal, five distinct game worlds, compact typography and confident controls. The inline hudl n game wordmark uses a tilted lime lowercase n card; a three-character cast drawn from the actual Identity component connects the default home and player roster, while individual game emblems distinguish the five choices. Cards feel like a deck shared around a table; private role information has immediate, unmistakable hierarchy.

Social Club is the default masked identity edition. Settings > Design > Social Club original restores the previous Social Club home and logo; Classic restores the original home, logo, Fredoka/Nunito typography and sticker-book styling. The original specification is preserved in [the Classic design archive](docs/design-archive/classic/DESIGN.md). The user-authorized replacement is recorded in [the surface brief](docs/surface.md) and current PRODUCT.md brand commitments; the underlying five-game product, player names and twenty stored avatar identifiers remain. The default edition renders those identifiers as deterministic masked SVG identities; the original editions retain their prior presentation.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Lime lobby actions, charcoal typography and five game-specific palettes and surface forms.
- Self-hosted Space Grotesk throughout the default design.
- Compact phone layouts, generous touch controls and a persistent lobby navigation.
- Flat controls with limited depth on decorative cards and dialogs.
- Twenty stable identities mapped to ten silhouettes and faces, with alternate cheek/accessory treatments and six colours; finite entry motion without replay controls.
- Reversible Social Club original and Classic variants, plus immediate hold-to-reveal privacy.

Documentation is extracted from `src/club.css`, `src/remix.css`, inherited rules in `src/style.css` and current React components. The current authority is “Five distinct game worlds” in `docs/surface.md`. The independent finish reviewer reports **ship**, with no material findings. Evidence is recorded in `docs/verification/worlds-review.md` and `.impeccable/review/worlds/`: a production build, 21 unit tests, all 18 browser tests and four scoped browser checks after the final CSS correction passed.
