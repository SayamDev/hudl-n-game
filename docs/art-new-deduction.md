# New deduction artwork

Both original illustrations were created using the built-in image-generation tool, visually reviewed, then exported to local WebP. No external image request occurs during play. Original covers for other games were preserved.

- `public/art/after-hours/spyfall.webp` — masked spy studying a folded map with a lime question-mark route; lobby and investigation/private cover scenes.
- `public/art/after-hours/secret-hitler.webp` — divided masked parliament, empty presidential chair and sealed ballot box; lobby and parliamentary handoffs. Part of the noncommercial CC BY-NC-SA adaptation; see secret-hitler-license.md.

## Prompts

Spyfall: Original polished square editorial cover for Hudl n Game. A mysterious ivory masked spy in a purple trench coat leaning over a folded city map, lime question-mark route, distant noir city silhouettes. Detailed linocut engraving, rich halftone grain, sharply defined illustrated faces and hands. Midnight, violet, acid lime and warm ivory. Theatrical secret society party mood, close composition for wide cropping, central spy face. No text, lettering, logos or existing game graphics.

Secret Hitler: Original square illustrated cover for Hudl n Game’s social deduction game. Divided circular parliament chamber, ivory masked politicians in violet suits facing one another, central sealed ballot box and shadowed empty presidential chair. Art deco arches, intricate linocut engraving and halftone print grain. Midnight, purple, ivory, small acid lime highlights and muted crimson ballots. No lettering, logos, Nazi insignia, real people or existing boardgame artwork.
