Changes: new original noir illustration, rewritten screen copy, shared-phone private role and policy handoffs, sequential sealed digital ballots revealed together, automated policy deck, executive powers, election tracker, eligibility checks and win resolution. Printed assets, original illustrations and rulebook prose are not reproduced. The digital implementation uses named recipients and an explicit privacy screen rather than physical envelopes.
