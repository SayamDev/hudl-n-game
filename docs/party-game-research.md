# Possible next games — 24 October 2026

The request was to research familiar additions, not to add unfinished lobby cards or copy another game's decks or artwork. These are references for potential original modes using Hudl's own content and illustrated identity.

| Direction | Reference | Fit for Hudl | Main work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Majority-answer guessing | [Herd Mentality, official publisher](https://bigpotato.com/products/herd-mentality) | Familiar prompts, private answers, then a suspenseful majority reveal; works with the chosen groups and shared phone. | Original prompt bank, answer entry/normalisation and clear tie/majority rules. Consider the pass-around time before choosing this mode. |
| Team spectrum guessing | [Wavelength, official publisher](https://www.cmyk.games/products/wavelength) | Two groups give clues and place a needle on a spectrum. Existing slider and protected reveal work offer a useful foundation. | Private target, original spectrum/clue content, shared guessing dial, turn/scoring rules and its own reviewed illustration. |
| Team word association | [Codenames, official publisher](https://www.czechgames.com/games/codenames) | Strong fit for word packs and teams; different pace from acting or hidden-role discussion. | Public word grid and a protected clue-giver key; test whether one shared phone is comfortable before committing to that model. |
| A compact one-night deduction round | [One Night Ultimate Werewolf, official publisher](https://beziergames.com/products/one-night-ultimate-werewolf) | Familiar hidden-role reference; rounds are shorter than traditional Mafia. | Role-change mechanics and a different win-resolution engine. Consider later because it overlaps most with existing Mafia. |

Recommended next exploration: an original spectrum game, followed by a majority-answer game. This is a product judgement about variety and reuse, not a claim that the commercial references can simply be bundled. Scope and art review should precede any new game implementation.

Sources were checked on 24 October 2026. No external game content, logos, question cards or illustrations were added to the app.
