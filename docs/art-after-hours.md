# After Hours artwork and type

## Generated illustration

`public/art/after-hours/club-illustrations.jpg` derives from candidate C of a text-only 12ui generation made for Hudl n game. Hosted generation ID: `crt-ab67535a7ea7377353cd942f41741d52ed00847e`. This is a run identifier, not a concept seed. No user screenshots or player data were uploaded for this generation.

NoirArt renders illustration regions with an SVG viewBox; the generated sheet's labels and sample counts are not app content. Controls, headings and player data are native DOM. The PNG was converted to JPEG locally. The exact generation prompt is embedded in the shipping JPEG; the provenance scan reported one raster and zero missing prompts. SHA-256 including metadata: `5c5371e88bd3f30680775646bceb790649f86245f2c199f09fac0fb98847f7ba`.

A paid hosted branch/conversion was blocked by automatic approval review. No paid expansion was performed; the implementation was completed locally.

## Fonts and implementation

Bebas Neue by Ryoichi Tsunekawa is self-hosted, with its SIL Open Font License in `public/fonts/bebas-neue/OFL.txt`. Space Grotesk retains its existing local OFL licence. Motion and Phosphor Icons are MIT-licensed dependencies. The sleeve, verdict planes and winning burst are locally authored geometry and animation code.

## References

[Rive game UI](https://rive.app/game-ui) informed state-driven motion. [ustwo's Monument Valley launch notes](https://ustwo.com/blog/monument-valley-out-now/) informed the coherence of art and interaction. The user's Fakeit reference informed the party-game context. These products' artwork was not copied or bundled. Rive is a reference, not an installed runtime.
