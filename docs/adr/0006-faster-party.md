# Faster rounds, helpful words and classic Charades

24 October 2026.

Quick group voting is the default for future rounds. A one-time options revision upgrades legacy defaults; an active session keeps its own voting mode and ballots. Secret pass-and-vote remains optional, with an explicit escape to quick voting. Group ties keep everyone in play for more discussion. The public verdict uses a swipeable masked envelope and an equivalent native button. There is no forced suspense timer, and identity content is not mounted until revealed. Reduced motion removes the envelope transition.

Imposter hints default to one familiar related word, alongside the category. Every starter entry is covered by authored associations (1,325 unique words/phrases across 1,329 entries). Category-only remains a harder option. Unknown custom words fall back honestly to their category rather than manufacturing an unrelated clue. Hint content is transient and is removed immediately when the hold ends. The offline dictionary requires no network or AI inference. Association candidates from an external dictionary were evaluated and rejected for poor relevance; shipped hints are manually authored.

Discussion seconds=0 means no timer: no running countdown, timer controls or countdown cues. Timed options remain available. Heads Up and classic Charades support the same no-timer choice.

Classic Charades shares the existing team scoring engine, with a distinct play-style option and a home shortcut. It requires at least four players for two teams. Each actor privately checks their prompt using the existing protected hold interaction, then hides it before acting silently. The prompt is absent from the acting screen. Correct/Skip rotates to the next actor on that team; the timed version pauses for private handoffs. Untimed turns finish manually. Two team turns determine the existing winner/points flow. Existing Heads Up retains its touch, keyboard and tilt controls.

Game art and card entrances trigger once when scrolled into view. The Imposter emblem uses the app's own geometric cast. This extends the established Social Club world and keeps both older design variants available.
