# ADR 0009: After Hours illustrated deck

Status: accepted, 24 October 2026.

The user requested a more distinctive, mature party app with intentional motion, recognisable alibis and online inspiration, while keeping phone reach and protected shared-device play.

Use an illustrated noir deck with ivory, midnight, lime and violet, self-hosted Bebas Neue display type, existing Space Grotesk controls and twenty detailed framed alibi emblems. Each game keeps a different accent and illustration within this shared vocabulary. Generated artwork is used only as decorative regions, never as a screenshot interface. All text, controls, states and counts remain real app content.

Public motion follows the game: a private sleeve fans into readiness, the active round rail tracks progress, a public verdict lifts away, and winners land as a group. No reveal animation delays private concealment. Secret text unmounts immediately on release/cancel/blur/Escape. A native public reveal button remains available alongside drag. Reduced motion avoids the physical transformations.

The existing deckStyle preference is the rollback: disabling After Hours art & motion restores the preceding Social Club treatment. Earlier design variants remain separately selectable. Game rules, stable player IDs and scores are unchanged.

12ui produced a text-only four-candidate direction; candidate C informed the implementation. A proposed paid hosted expansion was blocked by automatic review and was not retried. Integration continued locally using existing dependencies, the generated illustration and credited open-source assets. There is no approved pixel comp or formal concept-roll seed. Provenance and review boundaries are recorded separately.
